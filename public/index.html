Sample workflows

Illustrative examples of the tasks our reviewers handle and the outputs a pilot can produce.

Speech & music

Rate the naturalness of individual speech clips using Mean Opinion Score (MOS), or compare two versions using Comparative Mean Opinion Score (CMOS). For music, verify instrument tags and timestamped segment boundaries.

  • Sample output: clip scores, listening notes, and corrected timestamps.

Video annotation

Review AI-suggested action labels against the footage, correct start and end times, and flag hand–object interactions that are obscured in the frame.

  • Sample output: verified event labels with start and end times.

Model evaluation

Score candidate answers against a rubric, rank response pairs, and prepare corrected examples for supervised fine-tuning. Legal and medical reviewers can check answers against the supplied reference material.

  • Sample output: rubric scores, ranking rationales, and revised responses.
