Speech

Illustrative

Rate each clip for naturalness using Mean Opinion Score (MOS), then run A/B listening comparisons with audible reasons for every preference or tie.

Pilot input. Two voice renders of “Your delivery is scheduled for Friday.” Five listeners rate each clip independently, from 1 (very unnatural) to 5 (fully natural).

A/B comparison records (CMOS)

Model evaluation

Illustrative

Score each answer against the same rubric, rank the responses, and write a corrected training answer grounded in the supplied reference.

User prompt
“What format can I export my report in, and where do I find the option?”
Product guide (reference)

Evaluation taskScore two candidate model responses out of 5 on Grounding, Completeness, and Clarity, then rank and rewrite.
