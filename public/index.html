Speech

Rate each clip for naturalness using Mean Opinion Score (MOS). In a separate A/B test, compare versions of the same utterance and record an audible reason for every preference or tie.

Sample output illustrative

Pilot input. Two voice renders of “Your delivery is scheduled for Friday.” Five listeners rate each clip independently, from 1 (very unnatural) to 5 (fully natural).

Individual clip ratings

Clip A
5, 4, 4, 5, 4

Clip B
3, 3, 4, 2, 3
3.0
The final syllable of “Friday” gets unusually strong stress.

A/B comparison records (CMOS)

Each pair maps its clips to Audio A and Audio B. Positive scores favor A, negative scores favor B, and zero means no preference. Scores of +1 and −1 indicate a slight preference in this sample.

  • Clips A and B
    +1 · Audio A slightly better

    “Your delivery is scheduled for Friday.”

    Audio A · Clip A
    At 0:02.4, the first syllable of “Friday” carries the expected stress.
    Audio B · Clip B
    At 0:02.5, the second syllable gets stronger stress, making the word sound less natural.
  • Clips C and D
    −1 · Audio B slightly better

    “Please leave the package at the front desk.”

    Audio A · Clip C
    At 0:01.8, a short pause separates “the” from “front desk,” interrupting the phrase.
    Audio B · Clip D
    “The front desk” flows as one phrase. Both clips are intelligible, so the preference is slight.
  • Clips E and F
    0 · No preference

    “Your appointment starts at ten.”

    Audio A · Clip E
    Even pacing and clear pronunciation. A faint hiss follows the last word.
    Audio B · Clip F
    Pacing and pronunciation match Audio A closely, including the faint hiss at the end.

QA check. Confirm that the score direction matches the preferred audio and that every tie explains what sounds similar.

Delivered: per-clip mean scores, comparison scores, and listening notes that support each decision.

Model evaluation

Score each answer against the same rubric, rank the responses, and write a corrected answer that preserves what the source supports.

Sample output illustrative
User prompt

Grounding2

Ranking rationaleA gets the format and page right but omits the Export step. B adds unsupported PDF export and points to the wrong page.
Rewritten answer“CSV is the only supported format. Open Reports, select Export, then choose CSV.”

QA check. Grounding checks facts against the guide. Completeness checks both parts of the question. Clarity checks whether the instructions are easy to follow. With equal weights, A earns 14 out of 15 and B earns 8 out of 15.

Delivered: criterion-level scores, a ranked pair with rationale, and a corrected training answer.
